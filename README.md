\# Cert Practice Hub



Free, browser-based practice tests for IT certifications. The first exam included is \*\*Microsoft Fabric Data Engineer Associate (DP-700)\*\*.



\*\*Live site:\*\* https://undiscovered-genius.github.io/cert-practice-hub/



\## Features



\- 173 multiple-choice questions, split into practice sets of up to 50

\- Single-answer questions use radio buttons and multi-select questions use checkboxes

\- \*\*Check answers\*\* shows green for correct and red for wrong, with an explanation

\- \*\*Review answers\*\* after a test shows every question with your picks, the correct answers, and the reasons

\- Pass or fail result using an 80% pass mark

\- \*\*Score history\*\* with KPIs (attempts, average, best, pass rate, last-5 average), a trend chart of your last 10 scores, and accuracy by topic

\- Light and dark mode follow your device

\- No sign-up, no backend, and no tracking



\## Topics covered



Ingest \& Spark, Lakehouse \& Delta, Real-Time Intelligence, Data Warehouse, Security \& Governance, CI/CD \& Monitoring



\## How your history is stored



Scores are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. They stay after a refresh, but they don't sync between devices or browsers, and clearing your browser's site data removes them.



\## Run locally



Open `index.html` in any browser. There's nothing to install.



\## Hosting



The site runs on GitHub Pages: Settings > Pages > Deploy from branch > `main` / root.
